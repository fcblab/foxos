## Ponytail × Caveman Compatibility

- Ponytail governs implementation scope, simplicity, and engineering completeness.
- Caveman governs response wording and verbosity.
- Required disclosures take precedence over style rules.
- Skipped work, unverified checks, limitations, and material risks are mandatory disclosures, not recaps or closers.
- End implementation responses with 1–2 concise lines covering these disclosures.
- Never omit necessary information for brevity.
- No redundant summaries, filler, or generic closers.
- Never invent checks, results, or risks.
